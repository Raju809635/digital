# Digital Orbit – Night Mode
## Student User Manual

Digital Orbit turns exam questions into short, structured answers you can review on screen or save as notebook-style PDFs.

## Make your answer sheet

1. Open Digital Orbit in your browser.
2. Under **Upload important questions**, paste one or more questions. You can also upload a PDF that contains selectable text.
3. Choose **Pass Mode** for short, direct answers, or **Score Mode** for more detailed answers.
4. Select **Generate Handwritten Answers** and wait for your answers to appear below.

You can paste a subject or topic if you want the service to suggest a few foundational questions. Keep the request focused. Don’t enter passwords, private information, or anything you would not want processed by an online service.

## Read and listen

Select a question to expand or collapse its answer. Answers use a simple exam structure: definition, explanation, key points, and conclusion. The scoring keywords are listed in each answer for quick review.

Select **Listen · 2x** to hear an answer read aloud by your browser. Select **Pause audio** to stop. Speech playback depends on the voices available on your device and browser.

## Download PDFs

- **Download Handwritten PDF** saves all generated answers as notebook-style pages with blue handwritten-style text, underlined headings, and a red margin.
- **1-Page Revision PDF** saves a compact keyword sheet for a last-minute scan.

If a PDF upload does not work, it may be scanned as an image. This version reads selectable PDF text; for a scanned document, copy the questions into the text box instead. PDF uploads are limited to 12 MB and 30 pages.

## If something doesn’t work

- **The answer generator is busy** — wait a little and try again.
- **Your PDF has no selectable text** — paste the questions directly into the box.
- **No audio** — check your device volume and browser settings, or read the answer on screen.
- **A PDF doesn’t open** — check your browser downloads and open the saved file with a PDF reader.

Digital Orbit does not ask students for an AI provider API key. Service configuration is handled by the owner.
