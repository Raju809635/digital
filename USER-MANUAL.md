# Digital Orbit
## Student User Manual

Digital Orbit turns up to five exam questions into focused exam-style answers. Paste questions, upload a question-paper PDF, or upload lesson audio/video to make study notes, then choose the marks target.

## Start an answer set

1. Paste up to **5 questions**, preferably numbered or one per line, or upload a text-based question-paper PDF.
2. Review the extracted text in the question box after upload and edit it if a question or line was missed.
3. Choose **5 Marks** for a focused answer or **10 Marks** for a fuller answer. A 10-mark theory answer aims for about 2-3 handwritten pages when the question needs that much detail.
4. Select **Generate Handwritten Answers**.

Keep each batch at five questions or fewer for better, more focused answers. The count below the question box is an estimate; check the extracted text before generating.

## Read answers

Conceptual answers include relevant exam sections and diagrams when they help explain the exact question. Math questions use readable worked steps and a final answer without unrelated keyword or diagram sections. Expand a question to read it, or select **Listen** to hear the browser read it aloud.

Answers are AI-generated study material. Check formulas, examples, and syllabus-specific details against your class notes.

## Upload a PDF

Select **Upload PDF**. The app extracts selectable text and arranges it by the text's position on each page, then places the extracted text in the editable question box. Review it before generating answers.

PDF uploads are limited to 12 MB and 30 pages. Scanned/image-only PDFs do not contain selectable text, so they cannot be read yet; paste the questions manually in that case.

## Make notes from lesson audio or video

Choose **Audio / Video**, select an audio or video file, choose 5 or 10 marks, and select **Transcribe & Make Notes**. Captions are not needed; the app transcribes speech from the uploaded file. Uploads are limited to 25 MB. You can also use a YouTube link when the video has captions the app can access.

## Download

- **Download Handwritten PDF** saves the full set as notebook-style pages.
- **1-Page Revision PDF** saves a compact scan sheet. For math answers it includes the final result.

Digital Orbit does not ask students for an AI provider API key. Service configuration is handled by the owner.
